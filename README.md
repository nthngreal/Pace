# Pace

One-screen Android app: tap **Meal**, **Snack** or **Coffee**, and it tells you when you can eat or have the next coffee, how much coffee is left today, and when to stop before bed.

- Tap a button to log now, hold it to pick a time (past or planned).
- Tap an entry to change its time, ✕ to delete it, **Undo** to revert the last change.
- Settings: bedtime (default 22:00), caffeine per cup (default 110 mg ≈ 2 tsp instant), breaks after a meal (3 h) and a snack (2 h).
- Rules and sources are in the app under ⓘ: 400 mg/day (FDA), last coffee 9 h before bed (Gardiner et al. 2023), last food 3 h before bed (ACG 2022).

English UI, with Ukrainian and Russian translations. The APK is built by GitHub Actions and published to the `latest` release.
